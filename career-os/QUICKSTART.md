# Career OS — Quick Start

Welcome! Here's how to get value in the next five minutes. No internet or setup
is required to run the app.

## 1. Open it

Double-click **`index.html`**. It opens in your browser and runs entirely
offline.

> Tip: for the smoothest "Copy" behavior, you can also open it in a normal
> browser tab or serve the folder with any simple local web server — but
> double-clicking works fine.

## 2. Choose your edition

Top right, click the **edition switcher** and pick the field closest to yours —
General, Nursing, Tech, Education, or Executive. The whole app re-skins and every
tool starts speaking your industry's language. (You can also do this in
**Settings**.)

## 3. Build your résumé

- Sidebar → **Résumé Builder**.
- Fill in your details; watch the **live preview** update on the right.
- Add Experience/Education entries with **+ Add**, reorder with the arrows.
- Export with **Print / PDF**, **Copy text**, or **Download .doc**.
- It **autosaves** — come back any time.

## 4. Compose with a tool

- Pick any tool (e.g. **Build → Resume Bullets**, or **Interview → Mock
  Interview Simulator**).
- Fill the fields and press **Compose prompt**.
- Press **Copy prompt**, then **Open Claude** or **Open ChatGPT**, and paste.
- The AI returns your finished writing. (Press **View prompt** to see what was
  composed.)

## 5. Track your applications

- Sidebar → **Job Tracker**.
- **Add job** — it lands in *Wishlist*.
- **Drag** cards across Wishlist → Applied → Interview → Offer → Rejected.
- **Search** and **filter** to focus. It autosaves.

## Make it yours

- **Settings** → Day/Evening theme, accent & occupation, text size, autosave,
  and "Clear all saved data".
- **Bonuses** → power verbs, ATS rules, templates, and checklists.
- **Help** → FAQ and tips.

## One thing to remember

Your data lives **only in this browser on this device**. Export your résumé and
keep copies of anything important — especially before switching devices or
clearing your browser.

Happy job hunting. 🎯
