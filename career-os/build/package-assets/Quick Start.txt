CAREER OS — QUICK START
========================

Welcome! Here's how to get value in the next five minutes. No internet,
install, or account is required — everything runs entirely offline, right
in your browser.


1. OPEN IT

   Double-click "Career OS — Open This File.html" (the other file in this
   folder). It opens in your default web browser and works completely
   offline from that point on.

   Tip: if your computer asks which program to open it with, choose your
   web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).


2. CHOOSE YOUR EDITION

   Top right, click the edition switcher and pick the field closest to
   yours — General, Nursing, Tech, Education, or Executive. The whole app
   re-skins and every tool starts speaking your industry's language. (You
   can also change this later in Settings.)


3. BUILD YOUR RÉSUMÉ

   - Sidebar -> Résumé Builder
   - Fill in your details; watch the live preview update on the right
   - Add Experience/Education entries with "+ Add", reorder with the arrows
   - Export with Print / PDF, Copy text, or Download .doc
   - It autosaves — come back any time
   - Try "Load example" if you want to see a filled-in sample first


4. COMPOSE WITH A TOOL

   - Pick any tool (e.g. Build -> Resume Bullets, or Interview -> Mock
     Interview Simulator)
   - Fill the fields and press "Compose prompt"
   - Press "Copy prompt", then "Open Claude" or "Open ChatGPT", and paste
   - The AI returns your finished writing


5. TRACK YOUR APPLICATIONS

   - Sidebar -> Job Tracker
   - "Add job" — it lands in Wishlist
   - Drag cards across Wishlist -> Applied -> Interview -> Offer -> Rejected
   - Search and filter to focus. It autosaves. Export to CSV any time.


MAKE IT YOURS

   - Settings -> theme, accent & occupation, text size, autosave, and
     "Clear all saved data"
   - Bonuses -> power verbs, ATS rules, templates, and checklists
   - Help -> FAQ and tips (all inside the app)


ONE THING TO REMEMBER

   Your data lives only in this browser on this device. Export your résumé
   and keep copies of anything important — especially before switching
   devices or clearing your browser.

Happy job hunting.
