**Global specs.** 2000 × 1600 px (5:4, safe for Etsy's current display and zoom;
first image also reads well cropped square in search). JPG, sRGB. Brand kit:
midnight indigo `#232946` / deep night `#191D33`, warm ivory `#FAF6EE`, antique
gold `#A8894F` / soft gold `#C9B183`, terracotta `#C2775B`. Fonts: Cormorant
Garamond (display), Marcellus (kickers), Lato (support text). All page previews
are REAL renders of the exported PDFs (no mockup fakery, no AI hands, no fake
badges or reviews). Every image carries mobile-legible headline type (≥70 px
at export size) and generous margins; nothing important within 60 px of edges.
Repeated footer strip on images 2–15: tiny "GlowHausDigital · digital download"
so screenshots stay attributed and honest.

These images are generated programmatically by `build_listing_images.py` in
this folder; rerun it after any PDF change so previews stay accurate.
