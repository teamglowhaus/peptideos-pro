## Search phrases to avoid

Low return or wrong audience — do not spend title/tag space here:

- "manifestation" alone — Highly saturated; single words underperform (seller-education consensus)
- "journal" / "printable" / "PDF" alone — Highly saturated; zero specificity
- "law of attraction planner" — Highly saturated; attracts the most price-sensitive shelf
- "digital paper," "wallpaper," "clip art," "background" — wrong category; saturated image-set traffic that won't convert on a workbook
- "PLR," "resell," "MRR," "commercial use" — attracts resellers, not end users; also signals the low-trust shelf we're differentiating from
- "witchcraft," "spell," "grimoire" — real but different niche with different expectations; mixing signals confuses Etsy's audience matching (inference)
- "free," "cheap," "budget" — price-anchor poison for a premium listing

## Misleading phrases to avoid

Phrases that risk buyer disappointment, policy trouble, or factual embarrassment:

- "Sirius closest to Earth" / "rare cosmic alignment on 8/8" — Potentially misleading; astronomically false (Sirius is ~8.6 light-years away year-round; the 8/8 date is numerological)
- "ancient Egyptian Lion's Gate ritual" — Potentially misleading; no such documented ancient ritual; say "inspired by the ancient reverence for Sirius's return" instead
- "Giza pyramid alignment" — Potentially misleading; the claim lacks evidence
- "guaranteed manifestation," "manifest anything instantly," "proven to attract money" — outcome guarantees; ethically off-limits and FTC-risky
- "heals anxiety/depression," "therapy," "trauma healing" — implied medical claims; off-limits
- "Franklin Covey" as a naming keyword — trademark; describe compatibility factually in the description ("fits Classic-size 5.5 x 8.5 ring planners") rather than tagging the brand (inference/caution; standard trademark hygiene)
- "course" — only if there is genuinely sequenced instruction; ours qualifies as a guided experience, but avoid implying video lessons
- Inflated page counts or counting sticker/duplicate pages — a documented buyer grievance; state single-sided content pages honestly

## Title structure recommendation

**Verified limit (2026 seller-guide consensus): 140 characters.** Only roughly the first 40–50 characters display in search results, so the seasonal money phrase must lead. Recommended structure:

`[Primary seasonal phrase] + [product type] + [structure hook] + [formats] + [secondary seasonal] + [evergreen benefit]`

**Draft title (138 characters — verified count):**

> Lions Gate Portal 8/8 Manifestation Workbook, 8 Day Self Concept Journal, Printable + GoodNotes, August 8 Ritual, Leo Season Abundance PDF

Why this works: the first 44 characters ("Lions Gate Portal 8/8 Manifestation Workbook") are a complete, high-intent phrase that survives truncation; "8 Day Self Concept Journal" carries the differentiator and the evergreen cluster; formats reassure; the tail catches date-literal and Leo-season searches. After August 12, swap the lead: "8 Day Manifestation Workbook, Self Concept Journal… Lions Gate Portal 8/8" (keep the phrase in the listing year-round for next season's history).
