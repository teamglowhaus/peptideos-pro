Is this a physical product? No; it is a digital download. Nothing ships.

Are the holes punched or printed? Neither; ring spacing varies between binders, so the guide teaches the one-minute template method (borrow a punched page, mark, test-punch one sheet).

What if my ring spacing is different? The binding margins are wide and mirrored, so nearly any personal-binder punch pattern fits inside them.
