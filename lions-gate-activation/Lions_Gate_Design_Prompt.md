# DESIGN PROMPT — The Lion's Gate 8/8 Activation

Paste everything below the line into your design AI, and attach
`Lions_Gate_Complete_Manuscript.md` (the full written content). Work in
sections if the tool has length limits: front matter first, then one part at a
time. The prompt gives it the complete art direction so every batch matches.

---

You are a luxury editorial designer creating a premium spiritual workbook for
the Etsy shop **GlowHausDigital**. The complete manuscript is attached; your
job is design only — do not rewrite, shorten or "improve" the text. Typos
aside, every word is final. Notes in *(italic parentheses)* are layout
instructions (how many writing lines an exercise needs), not printed text.

## The product

**The Lion's Gate 8/8 Activation** — an eight-day manifestation, self-concept
and aligned-action workbook for spiritually curious women, sold as a digital
download. It must feel like a $60 keepsake book: something between a luxury
astrology journal and a beautifully art-directed hardcover self-development
book. The buyer should want to photograph it for Instagram.

## Mood, in one sentence

A golden doorway at dusk: quiet, warm, celestial and expensive — Vogue
editorial meets antique celestial atlas, never cute, never clip-art, never
neon-mystical.

## Palette (exact values)

- Deep midnight indigo `#232946` and deepest night `#191D33` — covers, part
  dividers, meditation accents
- Warm ivory `#FAF6EE` and luminous cream `#FDFBF4` — nearly all interior pages
- Muted antique gold `#A8894F` and soft gold `#C9B183` — rules, ornaments,
  small headings; use like real gold foil: thin, precise, sparing
- Burnished amber `#B97F42`, soft terracotta `#C2775B`, dusty rose `#C4958D`,
  subtle plum `#6C5677` — rare accents only (one per page at most)
- Body ink `#33303C`

Rule: at least 80% of writing pages keep light backgrounds (printer-friendly).
Dark pages are reserved for the cover, the 12 part dividers, and a few
meditation/ritual moments.

## Typography

- Headings: an elegant high-contrast editorial serif (Cormorant Garamond or
  similar — Playfair Display, Freight, or Canela-style). Generous size, tight
  elegant spacing, title case.
- Body: a clean humanist sans (Lato, or Karla/Figtree), 10.5–12 pt, never
  gray below 60% ink.
- Small caps / letterspaced labels (Marcellus-style) for section kickers,
  running heads and card categories.
- No script fonts for body text; a refined serif italic is the only accent.
- Fine print never below 8.5 pt.

## Signature artwork (this is where the magic must live)

Create a rich, hand-drawn-feeling illustration system — fine-line gold artwork
on indigo, and delicate single-weight line art on ivory. Think engraved
celestial atlas plates and art-nouveau borders, not flat geometric minimalism:

1. **The Gate** (hero motif): an ornate round-topped portal archway with
   layered moldings, keystones and star-studded coffers, a radiant sun-lion
   face at its crown — a majestic, serene lioness/lion rendered in flowing
   engraved linework with a mane that dissolves into sun rays and eight-point
   stars. This belongs on the cover, large and breathtaking.
2. Supporting motifs, drawn in the same engraved style: eight-point stars,
   Sirius as a brilliant multi-rayed star, delicate constellations with
   labeled-atlas charm, crescent moons, infinity lemniscates that read as a
   flowing figure-8 ribbon, sun faces, laurel and wheat botanicals (harvest =
   abundance), and thin double-rule frames with corner ornaments.
3. Texture: subtle paper grain on ivory pages; on indigo pages a faint
   nebula/gradient glow behind the gold linework so dark pages feel deep, not
   flat.

Never use: generic clip art, emoji-style icons, photographic stock, cartoon
lions, heavy drop shadows, or AI-artifact hands/faces.

## Page system (design families)

Design 8–10 master layouts and reuse them so the book feels unified but never
repetitive. Every page gets at least one subtle coordinated design element;
no plain text-on-white pages, and no decoration that steals writing space.

- **Cover**: full indigo, the ornate Gate illustration, title in large serif,
  brand line "GlowHausDigital", tagline "Eight days · Eight intentions · One
  aligned plan".
- **Part dividers (12)**: full indigo, one large motif each (gate, sun-lion,
  constellation, moon, infinity, laurel...), Roman numeral, part title,
  one-line subtitle in gold italic.
- **Education pages**: ivory, serif headline with short gold underline rule,
  elegant two-thirds text column, a small corner illustration.
- **Worksheet/journal pages**: cream, prompts inside thin gold-framed boxes
  with a tiny star at the corner, generous ruled writing lines (warm taupe,
  ~26 pt apart — real handwriting room), checklists with fine gold-outline
  boxes.
- **Eight day-pages**: each day opens with a medallion — the day number inside
  a radiant sun — plus the day name in large serif; morning/evening sections
  divided by thin rules; one affirmation set as a centered serif-italic
  showpiece.
- **Ritual pages**: soft rose/gold wash edges, numbered steps in gold circles,
  candle-safety notes in small print.
- **Meditation pages**: the darkest interior moments — indigo or deep plum
  with gold text and constellation art, generously leaded for slow reading.
- **Quote/affirmation display pages**: one line, huge serif, tiny ornament.

## Formats to produce (in this order)

1. Main workbook, US Letter (8.5 × 11 in), margins ≥ 0.75 in.
2. The same reflowed to A4 (210 × 297 mm) — reflow, don't scale.
3. Planner insert editions from the manuscript's condensed "Planner Edition"
   section — finished page sizes exactly: Pocket 3.5 × 6 in, Compact
   4.25 × 6.75 in, Classic 5.5 × 8.5 in, Monarch 8.5 × 11 in. Ring-side
   binding margins: Pocket 0.72 in, Compact 0.8 in, Classic 0.92 in, Monarch
   1.15 in, mirrored on facing pages; page numbers at the outer edge; no
   printed punch holes.
4. 32 affirmation cards at 3.5 × 2.5 in (8 per Letter sheet, cut marks,
   decorative card backs in the Gate style).
5. Optional: 12 phone wallpapers (1170 × 2532 px) using the affirmation lines
   marked in the manuscript, dark and light versions.

## Hard rules

- Keep every legal/licensing/disclaimer page and the Franklin Planner
  trademark disclaimer text exactly as written.
- Keep the seller placeholders visible: [INSERT WEBSITE OR ETSY SHOP URL],
  [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL], [INSERT YOUR FIRST NAME], [INSERT SOCIAL HANDLE].
- Writing space is sacred: where the manuscript says *(4 writing lines)*,
  give at least that much comfortable ruled space.
- High contrast always; no text over busy artwork; no ink-heavy interior
  pages; nothing enters the planner punch zones.
- Export print-quality PDF (vector text or 300 dpi), fonts embedded.

## Quality bar

Before calling any page done, ask: would this page survive being posted alone
on Pinterest as an ad for the product? If it looks like a Word document with a
gold line on it, it is not finished. The buyer is paying for the feeling of
walking through a golden gate — every page should carry a little of that.

Start with: the cover, one part divider, one education page, one day-page
spread, and one worksheet — show me those five as the style proof before
designing the rest.
